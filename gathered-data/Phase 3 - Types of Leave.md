# **CSC Leave Types Reference**

   
Consolidated from the Omnibus Rules on Leave (Rule XVI, Omnibus Rules Implementing Book V of E.O. No. 292 / CSC MC No. 41, s. 1998, as amended), the Amendment to the Omnibus Rules on Leave (CSC Resolution No. 2100020, 07 January 2021), and the leave types enumerated in CS Form No. 6 (Revised 2020).  
   
Leave types are ordered exactly as they appear on CS Form No. 6, Item 6.A. Vacation and sick leave are the only two types that accrue with service, so the next section deals with how those credits are earned.  
   
---

 

## **How Leave Credits Are Earned**

   
Only vacation leave and sick leave are *earned*. They accrue with service and accumulate without limit. Every other leave type on CS Form No. 6 is a fixed statutory entitlement granted per year, per event, or per instance, and is neither earned nor accumulated. This section covers the accrual rules in Sections 26–34, 43–48 of Rule XVI.  
 

### **The basic rule**

   
**One (1) day of vacation leave and one (1) day of sick leave for every twenty-four (24) days of actual service.**  
   
That works out to 1.25 days of each per month, or 15 days of each per year, which matches the entitlement in Section 1\. Vacation and sick leave accrue separately and in parallel: the same 24 days of service earn 1 day of each, not 1 day split between them.

### **What counts as “actual service”**

“Actual service” is the period of continuous service since the appointment of the employee, including periods covered by any previously approved leave with pay. So an employee on approved vacation or sick leave with pay keeps earning credits during that leave.  
 

> * **Leave without pay for any reason other than illness is NOT counted** as part of actual service, and therefore earns no credits.

 

> * For employees paid on a daily wage basis, Saturdays, Sundays and holidays falling within a period of service **are counted** as service, even though no pay was received on those days, since their service was not required then.

 

> * **Rounding rule:** a fraction of one-fourth or more but less than three-fourths is counted as one-half day; a fraction of three-fourths or more is counted as one full day.

### **What reduces or suspends accrual**

 

> * **Tardiness and undertime** are deducted against vacation leave credits only, never against sick leave, unless the undertime is for health reasons supported by a medical certificate and a leave application.

 

> * **Leave without pay** for reasons other than illness does not count as actual service; use Table III to compute the reduced monthly accrual.

 

> * **Rehabilitation privilege:** the absence is not deducted from VL/SL credits, but the employee **does not earn** VL or SL credits while on it.

 

> * **Terminal leave:** the employee earns no leave credits, being already out of the service.

 

> * **Secondment:** the seconded employee is on leave without pay from the mother agency but still earns leave credits, which are commutable immediately after the secondment and payable by the receiving agency.

 

> * **Probationary period:** an employee still on probation may already use whatever credits have been earned. Any LWOP incurred during probation extends the probationary period by the same number of days.

 

### **Situations that do *not* cost the employee credits**

 

> * **Work suspension announced after office hours have started:** the employee who failed to report that day is not marked absent for the whole day. Only the time from the start of official working hours up to the announcement is deducted.

 

> * **Absence immediately before or after a Saturday, Sunday or holiday:** the employee is not considered absent on those non-working days and **no leave credits are deducted** for them. He simply receives no salary for those days.

 

> * **Irregular work schedules** (e.g. hospital personnel who work weekends and are off-duty on other days): off-duty days falling within the period of authorized leave are excluded from the deduction, regardless of whether they land on a Saturday, Sunday or holiday.

 

> * **Flexible working hours:** where an employee renders less than 8 hours a day but completes 40 hours a week, only the hours of the prescribed core hours not served are deducted, not a full 8-hour day. Compensatory service may be rendered outside regular working hours (except Sundays) to offset non-attendance or undertime, subject to written approval and the agency’s own internal regulations.

 

### **Accumulation, carry-over and transfer**

 

> * Vacation and sick leave are cumulative. Any portion not taken within the calendar year carries over to succeeding years, with no ceiling on the number of days that may be accumulated.

 

> * On retirement, voluntary resignation, or separation through no fault of the employee, all accumulated VL and SL credits may be commuted (see Terminal Leave), exclusive of Saturdays, Sundays and holidays, regardless of when the credits were earned.

 

> * Where leave credits were commuted upon separation and the person is re-employed in government before the commuted leave expires, no refund of the money value is required, but leave credits restart at zero.

 

> * **Transfer between agencies:** an employee who transfers from one government agency to another may either have accumulated VL/SL credits commuted or transferred to the new agency. The transfer option is a matter of right only for an employee with no gaps in service; a gap of not more than one month is allowed if it was not the employee’s fault. The option must be exercised within one (1) year from the transfer. It does not apply to uniformed personnel moving from the military to the civilian service.

 

> * **Remedy if transfer is denied:** the employee may claim the money value of those credits from the office where they were earned.

 

> * **Salary during leave with pay** is the salary the employee is currently receiving. The head of agency may, at his discretion, authorize commutation of that salary and direct its payment at the beginning of the leave.

 

### **Teachers: converting service credits to VL/SL and back**

   
For teachers and school personnel on the teachers’ leave basis who resigned, retired, or were separated through no fault of their own on or after 16 January 1986:  
   
VL \+ SL  \=  (30 × Y) ÷ 69  
   
where 30 \= days in a month, Y \= total teacher’s service credits, and 69 \= 58 days of summer vacation \+ 11 days of Christmas vacation (derived as 84 total vacation days − 15 days for Saturdays/Sundays during summer and Christmas Day, Rizal Day and New Year’s Day). The resulting number of days is divided equally into vacation and sick leave.  
   
The reverse conversion, from VL/SL back into teacher’s service credits, is:  
   
Y  \=  \[(VL \+ SL) ÷ 30\] × 69  
 

### **Worked example**

   
An employee is appointed on March 1\. By December 31 of the same year (10 months of continuous service, no LWOP), the balances are:  
 

> * Vacation leave: 10 × 1.25 \= 12.50 days

 

> * Sick leave: 10 × 1.25 \= 12.50 days

   
Suppose in November the employee had exhausted all vacation leave credits and incurred 4 days of LWOP. November’s accrual drops from 1.25 to 1.083 days each (Table III, 26 days present), so the year-end balances become 12.333 days of each, before deducting any leave actually taken.  
   
If that same employee is late by a cumulative 45 minutes in a month, 0.094 of a day is deducted from vacation leave only (Table IV).

## **1\. Vacation Leave**

   
Leave of absence granted to officials and employees for personal reasons, the approval of which is contingent upon the necessities of the service.  
   
**Key Benefits and Conditions**  
 

> * **Duration:** 15 days with full pay per year, earned at 1.25 days per month (1 day of vacation leave for every 24 days of actual service), exclusive of Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.

 

> * **Service Requirement:** None. Entitlement begins upon entry into the service. Part-time employees earn proportionately (e.g. 4 hours/day for 5 days a week \= 7.5 days per year). Employees on a rotation basis earn based on the periods of service actually rendered.

 

> * **Usage:** For any personal reason other than illness. Filed on CS Form No. 6 at least 5 days in advance of the effective date, whenever possible, for absences of one (1) full day or more.

 

> * **Limitations:** Cumulative with no ceiling on accumulation, but approval is discretionary on the part of the head of agency and depends on the needs of the service. Tardiness and undertime are deducted against vacation leave credits. Absences in excess of earned credits become leave without pay. Five (5) days of the annual vacation leave are mandatory (see Mandatory/Forced Leave).

   
**Who Qualifies for this leave?**  
 

> * Appointive officials up to the level of heads of executive departments, undersecretaries, and all government employees, whether permanent, temporary, or casual, who render work during prescribed office hours.

 

> * Contractual employees, who are likewise entitled to vacation and sick leave credits.

 

> * Local elective officials (effective 12 May 1983 under B.P. 337 and R.A. 7160); their credits are both cumulative and commutative.

 

> * Part-time employees and employees on rotation basis, on a proportionate basis.

 

> * **Not covered:** Teachers, who receive proportional vacation pay (70 days summer \+ 14 days Christmas) instead, and officials/employees covered by special leave laws (justices, judges, foreign service officers, SUC faculty under R.A. 8292, etc.). Teachers designated to perform non-teaching functions with the same hours of service as regular employees *are* entitled to vacation and sick leave.

   
**Application Requirements:**  
 

> * Provide steps:

  1. Accomplish CS Form No. 6 (Revised 2020), checking “Vacation Leave” under Item 6.A, and indicate under Item 6.B whether the leave will be spent within the Philippines or abroad (specify).

  2. State the number of working days applied for and the inclusive dates under Item 6.C, and indicate under Item 6.D whether commutation is requested or not requested.

  3. File with the proper head of agency at least 5 days in advance of the effective date, whenever possible.

  4. HR certifies the leave credit balance (total earned, less this application, balance) under Item 7.A.

  5. Immediate supervisor recommends approval or disapproval under Item 7.B; the authorized official approves or disapproves under Items 7.C / 7.D.

  6. If the application is not acted upon within 5 working days from receipt, it is deemed approved.

## **2\. Mandatory/Forced Leave**

   
The portion of the annual vacation leave that employees are *required* to take, so that they actually rest during the year.  
   
**Key Benefits and Conditions**  
 

> * **Duration:** A minimum of 5 working days annually, charged against vacation leave credits. May be taken continuously or intermittently.

 

> * **Service Requirement:** Required of all officials and employees who have accumulated 10 days or more of vacation leave credits. Those with less than 10 days are not allowed to apply for Forced Leave.

 

> * **Usage:** The head of agency prepares a staggered schedule after prior consultation with employees. The head of agency may cancel any previously scheduled leave in the exigency of the service.

 

> * **Limitations:** Forfeited if not taken within the year. However, where the scheduled leave was cancelled by the head of agency in the exigency of the service, the unused leave is *not* deducted from accumulated vacation leave. Employees with 15 days of vacation leave who monetized 10 days are still required to go on forced leave.

   
**Who Qualifies for this leave?**  
 

> * All officials and employees with 10 or more days of accumulated vacation leave credits. For them it is mandatory, not optional.

 

> * Employees with less than 10 days of accumulated vacation leave credits may still opt to avail of it.

 

> * Retirement or resignation during the year without completing the calendar year does not warrant forfeiture of the corresponding leave credits if the employee opted not to avail of the mandatory five-day leave.

   
**Application Requirements:**  
 

> * Provide steps:

  1. Coordinate with HR/the head of agency on the agency’s staggered schedule of mandatory leave for the year.

  2. Accomplish CS Form No. 6, checking “Mandatory/Forced Leave” under Item 6.A.

  3. Fill out Item 6.B (within the Philippines / abroad), the number of working days and inclusive dates under Item 6.C, and commutation under Item 6.D.

  4. Submit to the head of agency following the same routing as vacation leave (HR certification, recommendation, approval).

## **3\. Sick Leave**

   
Leave of absence is granted only on account of sickness or disability on the part of the employee or of any member of the employee’s immediate family.  
   
**Key Benefits and Conditions**  
 

> * **Duration:** 15 days with full pay per year, earned at 1.25 days per month (1 day of sick leave for every 24 days of actual service), exclusive of Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays.

 

> * **Service Requirement:** None. Entitlement begins upon entry into the service; part-time and rotation-basis employees earn proportionately.

 

> * **Usage:** Filed immediately upon the employee’s return from leave, with notice of absence sent to the immediate supervisor and/or agency head at the time of the absence. May be filed in advance where the employee will undergo medical examination or an operation, or is advised to rest due to ill health, supported by a medical certificate.

 

> * **Limitations:** Applications in excess of 5 successive days must be accompanied by a proper medical certificate. For 5 days or less, the head of agency determines whether the grant is proper and may require a medical certificate in case of doubt. Approval is *mandatory* once proof of sickness or disability is attached. Unreasonable delay or unjustified non-approval is a ground for sanction against the approving official. When sick leave credits are exhausted, vacation leave credits may be used; the reverse is not allowed.

> 

> * If sick leave is already exhausted, absences can be charged to VL, but not vice-versa.

   
**Who Qualifies for this leave?**  
 

> * All officials and employees entitled to leave privileges under Section 1, Rule XVI: permanent, temporary, casual, and contractual.

 

> * “Immediate family” for purposes of sick leave means the spouse, children, parents, unmarried brothers and sisters, and any relative living under the same roof or dependent upon the employee for support.

 

> * Part-time employees and employees on rotation basis, proportionately.

 

> * **Not covered:** Teachers (who instead use vacation service credits to offset absences due to illness) and those covered by special leave laws.

   
**Application Requirements:**  
 

> * Provide steps:

  1. Notify the immediate supervisor and/or agency head of the absence as soon as practicable.

  2. Accomplish CS Form No. 6, checking “Sick Leave” under Item 6.A.

  3. Under Item 6.B, indicate whether the illness was treated in hospital or as an out patient, and specify the illness.

  4. Attach a medical certificate if the sick leave exceeds 5 successive days, or if required by the head of agency in case of doubt. The certificate must bear the complete details of the physician (name, PRC/license, PTR and S2 numbers), the employee, and the employee’s condition. CS Form No. 41 is no longer used.

  5. File the application immediately upon return to work (or in advance, for scheduled medical examinations or operations).

  6. HR certifies leave credits; the approving authority acts on the application.

## **4\. Maternity Leave**

   
Leave of absence for pregnant female government employees of 105 days with full pay, to give them transition time to regain health and overall wellness and to assume maternal roles before resuming paid work. *(R.A. No. 11210, the 105-Day Expanded Maternity Leave Law, as implemented by CSC Resolution No. 2100020, s. 2021.)*  
   
**Key Benefits and Conditions**  
 

> * **Duration:**

  * **105 days with full pay** for live childbirth, regardless of mode of delivery (normal or caesarian).

  * **\+15 days with full pay** (total 120 days) if the female employee qualifies as a solo parent under R.A. No. 8972\.

  * **60 days with full pay** for miscarriage or emergency termination of pregnancy.

  * **\+30 days extended maternity leave** without pay, or with pay charged against sick leave credits (and, if exhausted, vacation leave credits), available only in cases of live childbirth.  
    * It can affect the employee’s service record which should not appear the gap in the service.

  * **Up to 7 days allocable** to the child’s father or, in his death/absence/incapacity, to an alternate caregiver. This is over and above the father’s own 7-day paternity leave.  
    * Not only limited to spouse, it can also be given to any primary carer like parents, sibling, etc.

> * **Service Requirement:** None. Granted regardless of civil status, employment status, length of service, and the legitimacy of the child.  
  * Applicable to COS/JO employees but the agency will not cover the salary.  
  * COS/JO employee’s DTR will not reflect maternity leave.  
  * SSS will cover the salary.

> * **Usage:** Prior written notice to the authorized officer at least 30 days in advance whenever possible, specifying the effectiveness of the leave. Enjoyed in a continuous and uninterrupted manner; part of it may be availed of on or before the actual date of delivery. Notice for the 30-day extension must be in writing and submitted at least 45 days before the end of the maternity leave (no prior notice needed in a medical emergency, but subsequent notice must be given). Pre-natal care covers not more than 45 calendar days before delivery; post-natal care not less than 60 calendar days after delivery.

> * **Limitations:** Non-cumulative and non-commutative (not convertible to cash); enjoyment cannot be deferred. Only one maternity benefit is paid per childbirth/delivery regardless of the number of offspring. The option to allocate does not apply to miscarriage or emergency termination of pregnancy. The 30-day extended leave without pay is not considered a gap in service. Availment cannot be used as a basis for diminution in rank, status or salary, or for termination.

   
**Who Qualifies for this leave?**  
 

> * Every female employee in the government service, regardless of civil status, employment status (permanent, temporary, provisional, coterminous, fixed term, substitute, casual or contractual), and length of service.

 

> * Female teachers, who may avail even during summer and Christmas vacations, in which case both the maternity leave benefits and the proportional vacation pay are granted.

 

> * A female employee with a pending administrative case, including one under preventive suspension or serving a suspension penalty (she must serve the unexpired portion of the suspension afterwards). **Not** entitled if a decision dismissing her from the service has become final and executory.

 

> * A female employee whose childbirth, miscarriage or emergency termination of pregnancy occurs not more than 15 calendar days after termination of her service. In cases of *illegal* termination, the 15-day prescriptive period does not apply and the full 105 or 60 days must be paid.

 

> * Granted in every instance of pregnancy, regardless of frequency; overlapping claims are granted consecutively.

   
**Application Requirements:**  
 

> * Provide steps:

  1. Give prior notice of pregnancy and intended availability to the authorized officer of the agency at least 30 days in advance whenever possible.


  2. Submission of CSC Clearance.


  3. Proof of Pregnancy (Ultrasound, Doctor’s/Medical Certificate).

  4. Accomplish CS Form No. 6 (Revised 2020), checking “Maternity Leave” under Item 6.A, and attach a medical certificate.

  5. If claiming the additional 15 days as a solo parent, attach the Solo Parent Identification Card.

  6. If allocating up to 7 days, accomplish CS Form No. 6a, s. 2020 (Notice of Allocation of Maternity Leave) and submit it together with the Application for Leave and proof of relationship. The father or alternate caregiver files his own CS Form No. 6 with his agency.

  7. To extend by 30 days after live childbirth, submit written notice to the authorized officer at least 45 days before the end of the maternity leave, indicating whether the extension is without pay or charged against sick/vacation leave credits.

  8. Any dispute on payment of maternity leave with full pay is filed first with the head of agency, appealable to the CSC Regional Office with jurisdiction over the agency and then to the Commission Proper. The agency may not hold or delay payment while a dispute is pending.

## **5\. Paternity Leave**

   
Leave of absence for married male government employees of 7 days with full pay, to enable them to lend care and support to their legal spouse before, during and after childbirth and to assist in rearing the newborn child. *(R.A. No. 8187, the Paternity Leave Act of 1996 / CSC MC No. 71, s. 1998, as amended.)*  
   
**Key Benefits and Conditions**  
 

> * **Duration:** 7 working days with full pay per delivery, for the first four (4) deliveries, whether childbirth or miscarriage, of the legitimate spouse.

 

> * **Service Requirement:** None as to length of service, but the employee must be married to and cohabiting with the spouse who delivers.

 

> * **Usage:** Filed within a reasonable period, about one (1) week, prior to the expected delivery, except in cases of miscarriage and abnormal deliveries. May be enjoyed continuously or intermittently on the days immediately before, during and after the delivery, but not later than the period of the maternity leave availed of by the spouse.  
  * Can be filed after the affected days.  
  * Can be filed after the maximum of 5 working days.  
  * Can be applied staggered within one month within the delivery date.

 

> * **Limitations:** Absolute maximum of 4 deliveries, reckoned from the effectiveness of the Paternity Leave Act on 15 July 1996\. A male employee with more than one legal spouse is still limited to 4 deliveries in total, regardless of which spouse delivers. Non-cumulative and strictly non-convertible to cash. Approval is mandatory on the part of the approving authority, unless the employee’s services are urgently needed to preserve life and property, in which case he is entitled to overtime pay instead.

   
**Who Qualifies for this leave?**  
 

> * Every married male employee in the government service, regardless of employment status and length of service.

 

> * Only for deliveries (childbirth or miscarriage) of his legitimate spouse with whom he is cohabiting.

 

> * Separate from, and in addition to, any allocated maternity leave credits (up to 7 days) received from the child’s mother.

   
**Application Requirements:**  
 

> * Provide steps:  
  1. Accomplish CS Form No. 6 (Revised 2020), checking “Paternity Leave” under Item 6.A.

  2. Marriage Contract


  3. Medical Certificate stating the date of delivery prior to the availment.


  4. Child’s Birth Certificate if applied after the delivery.


  5. File within a reasonable period (1) week, before the expected date of delivery, except for miscarriage and abnormal deliveries, which may be filed after.

  6. Indicate the number of working days applied for and the inclusive dates, aligned so that the leave falls not later than the period of maternity leave availed of by the spouse.

  7. On request of the office, furnish a certified true copy of the marriage contract, the birth certificate of the newborn child, or, in case of miscarriage, a medical certificate with pathology report duly signed by the attending physician or midwife showing the actual date of delivery.

## **6\. Special Leave Privilege**

   
A pool of up to three (3) days a year that employees may use for personal milestones, family obligations, domestic emergencies, personal transactions, and calamities, on top of vacation, sick, maternity and paternity leave.  
   
**Key Benefits and Conditions**  
 

> * **Duration:** A maximum of 3 days in a given year. The employee may take one special privilege leave of 3 days, or any combination of the qualifying occasions totalling not more than 3 days.

 

> * **Service Requirement:** None. Available with or without an existing or approved Collective Negotiation Agreement (CNA).

 

> * **Usage:** Available for any of the following (can be used as a dropdown in HRMS):   
  * (a) **personal milestones** such as birthdays, weddings, wedding anniversaries and other similar milestones including death anniversaries; 

  * (b) **parental obligations** such as school programs, PTA meetings, graduations, first communion, and medical needs where the employee’s child is involved; 

  * (c) **filial obligations** covering the employee’s moral obligation toward parents and siblings for their medical and social needs; 

  * (d) **domestic emergencies** such as sudden urgent home repairs or the sudden absence of a household helper; 

  * (e) **personal transactions** with government and private offices such as paying taxes, court appearances, or arranging a housing loan;

  *  (f) **calamity, accident and hospitalization** leave for force majeure events affecting the life, limb and property of the employee or immediate family.

 

> * **Limitations:** The three-day limit per year is strictly observed. Non-cumulative and strictly non-convertible to cash. Employees are no longer required to present proof of entitlement. It cannot be credited as compliance with the 7-day solo parent leave.

   
**Who Qualifies for this leave?**  
 

> * All officials and employees, with or without an existing or approved CNA.

 

> * Contractual employees, who are expressly entitled to the special leave privileges under Section 21\.

 

> * **Not covered:** Teachers and those covered by special leave laws.

 

> * “Immediate family” for the calamity/accident/hospitalization ground means the spouse, children, parents, unmarried brothers and sisters, or any relative living under the same roof or dependent on the employee for support.

   
**Application Requirements:**  
 

> * Provide steps:

  1. Accomplish CS Form No. 6, checking “Special Privilege Leave” under Item 6.A.

  2. Under Item 6.B, complete the “In case of Vacation/Special Privilege Leave” portion, indicating whether the leave will be spent within the Philippines or abroad.

  3. Indicate the number of working days and inclusive dates, keeping the running total for the year at or below 3 days.

  4. Submit to the head of the agency for approval. No proof of the occasion needs to be attached.

  5. If the birthday or wedding anniversary falls on a Saturday, Sunday or holiday, schedule the leave either before or after the occasion. The entitlement is not lost.

## **7\. Solo Parent Leave (Parental Leave)**

   
Leave benefits granted to a solo parent to perform parental duties and responsibilities where physical presence is required. *(R.A. No. 8972, the Solo Parents’ Welfare Act of 2000 / CSC MC No. 8, s. 2004.)*  
   
**Key Benefits and Conditions**  
 

> * **Duration:** 7 working days per year, in addition to existing leave privileges.

 

> * **Service Requirement:** At least one (1) year of government service, whether continuous or broken, regardless of employment status.

 

> * **Usage:** Availed on a staggered or continuous basis, subject to the approval of the head of agency. The application must be submitted at least one (1) week prior to availment, except in emergency cases. Grounds include attending to a child’s personal milestones (birthdays, first communion, graduation); performing parental obligations such as enrollment, school programs and PTA meetings; attending to the child’s medical, social, spiritual and recreational needs; and other similar circumstances requiring a parent’s physical presence.

> 

  * Can be use after the affected working days.  
  * Files within the maximum 5 working days.

 

> * **Limitations:** Must be availed every year. If not used within the calendar year it is forfeited. Not convertible to cash unless specifically agreed upon previously. Privileges terminate when the employee’s status changes (e.g. marriage, or the parent is no longer solely responsible for parenthood), without prejudice to re-application should circumstances warrant. Contingency/emergency leave under an agency policy or CNA, and the 3-day special privilege leave, cannot be credited as compliance with this benefit. Falsification of supporting documents or misrepresentation makes the employee administratively liable, without prejudice to criminal liability.

   
**Who Qualifies for this leave?**  
 

> * A woman who gives birth as a result of rape or other crimes against chastity, even without final conviction of the offender, provided she keeps and raises the child.

 

> * A parent left solo with the responsibility of parenthood due to: death of the spouse; detention or service of sentence of the spouse for a criminal conviction of at least one (1) year; physical or mental incapacity of the spouse as certified by a public medical practitioner; legal or de facto separation from the spouse for at least one year with custody of the children; declaration of nullity or annulment of marriage by a court or church with custody of the children; or abandonment by the spouse for at least one year.

 

> * An unmarried person who has preferred to keep and rear the children instead of having others care for them or giving them up to a welfare institution.

 

> * Any person who solely provides parental care and support to a child, provided he/she is a DSWD-licensed foster parent or a court-appointed legal guardian.

 

> * Any family member who assumes the responsibility of head of family due to the death, abandonment, disappearance or prolonged absence (at least one year) of the parents or solo parent.

 

> * “Children” means those living with and dependent on the solo parent for support who are unmarried, unemployed and below 18 years of age, or 18 and above but incapable of self-support and/or mentally and/or physically challenged.

   
**Application Requirements:**  
 

> * Provide steps:  
  1. Secure a Solo Parent Identification Card from the City/Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office. The card is valid for one year from issuance and must be renewed yearly. Failure to renew means the solo parent status has ceased or changed.

  2. Accomplish CS Form No. 6, checking “Solo Parent Leave” under Item 6.A.

  3. Attach certified true copies of the Solo Parent ID and the birth certificate(s) of the child/children, plus any other requirement such as a medical certificate where necessary.


  4. Birth Certificate of the child If the child is up to 22 years old.

  5. Submit at least one (1) week prior to availment, except in emergency cases.


  6. For legal guardian, Certificate from CSWDD that the employee is the assigned legal guardian of the child.

  7. The immediate supervisor/division chief recommends approval or disapproval based on the supporting documents; the head of agency approves or disapproves. Approval is mandatory if the application is in order. Non-approval without justifiable reason is a ground for sanction against the official concerned.

  8. The HRMO evaluates the application, verifies the authenticity of documents, monitors the employee’s solo parent status, and records the availability.

   
**Note for current implementation:** R.A. No. 11861 (Expanded Solo Parents Welfare Act, 2022\) broadened the definition of a solo parent but kept the parental leave at 7 days. The CSC issuance implementing it carries the current eligibility list, so check that before you build the eligibility rules.

## **8\. Study Leave**

   
A time-off from work with pay for qualified officials and employees to prepare for their bar or board examinations or to complete their master’s degree.  
   
**Key Benefits and Conditions**  
 

> * **Duration:** Not exceeding 6 months with pay for bar/board examination review; not exceeding 4 months for completion of a master’s degree.

 

> * **Service Requirement:** At least two (2) years of service with at least Very Satisfactory performance for the last two rating periods immediately preceding the application, and a permanent appointment (coterminous employees may be allowed under added conditions).

 

> * **Usage:** Covered by a written contract between the agency head or authorized representative and the employee, which fixes the service obligation: 1 month of leave \= 6 months service; 2–3 months \= 1 year; more than 3 up to 6 months \= 2 years.

 

> * **Limitations:** No extension is allowed once the maximum period has been availed; employees needing more time must file leave chargeable against vacation leave credits. Beneficiaries who fail to render the full service obligation due to voluntary resignation, optional retirement, expiration of a coterminous appointment, separation through their own fault, or other causes within their control must refund gross salary, allowances and benefits received while on study leave, proportionate to the unserved balance, using: R \= (SOR − SOS) / SOR × TCR. Beneficiaries who fail to take the bar/board exam or complete the master’s degree must inform the agency in writing through the personnel office and refund all salaries and benefits received; a repetition for causes within their control bars them from future study leave and training/scholarship grants.

   
**Who Qualifies for this leave?**  
 

> * Officials and employees who hold a bachelor’s degree that requires passing the bar or a board licensure examination for the practice of the profession.

 

> * Those whose profession or field of study is relevant to the agency’s mandate or to their own duties and responsibilities, as determined by the agency head.

 

> * Those with at least 2 years of service and Very Satisfactory ratings for the last two rating periods.

 

> * Those with no pending administrative and/or criminal charges.

 

> * Those with no current foreign or local scholarship grant.

 

> * Those who have fulfilled the service obligation of any previous training/scholarship/study leave grant.

 

> * Those with a permanent appointment. Coterminous employees may qualify if they meet all of the above, can fulfil the service obligation, and are not related within the 4th degree of consanguinity or affinity to the head of agency or any member of the collegial body/board.

 

> * **Not covered:** Those in the teaching profession, whose study leave and indefinite sick leave are governed by Sections 24 and 25 of R.A. 4670 (Magna Carta for Public School Teachers).

   
**Application Requirements:**  
 

> * Provide steps:

  1. Accomplish CS Form No. 6, checking “Study Leave” under Item 6.A.

  2. Under Item 6.B, tick the purpose, either Completion of Master’s Degree or BAR/Board Examination Review, or specify another purpose.

  3. Attach proof of the degree program or review enrollment.


  4. Performance ratings for the last two rating periods.


  5. Certification of no pending administrative or criminal charges. 

  6. Execute the study leave contract with the agency head or authorized representative, stating the period of grant and the corresponding service obligation.

  7. Comply with the agency’s own internal rules of procedure for equitable and rational availment of study leave.

  8. After the leave, inform the agency in writing through the personnel office of the result, including any failure to take the examination or complete the degree.

## **9\. 10-Day VAWC Leave**

   
Paid leave of absence for a woman employee who is a victim of violence, or whose child is a victim of violence, so she can attend to medical and legal concerns. *(R.A. No. 9262, the Anti-Violence Against Women and Their Children Act of 2004 / CSC MC No. 15, s. 2005.)*  
   
**Key Benefits and Conditions**  
 

> * **Duration:** Up to 10 days of paid leave, in addition to other paid leaves under the Labor Code and Civil Service rules. Extendible when necessity arises as specified in the protection order.

 

> * **Service Requirement:** None. Available regardless of employment status.

 

> * **Usage:** Availed in a continuous or intermittent manner to cover the days needed to attend to medical and legal concerns. Filed, whenever practicable, before the actual leave of absence, or immediately upon the woman employee’s return from the leave.

 

> * **Limitations:** Non-cumulative and not convertible to cash; leaves not availed of are considered waived. Any extension beyond 10 days must be charged against other leave benefits under civil service rules. An official who denies the application despite submission of a BPO/TPO/PPO is liable for conduct prejudicial to the best interest of the service.

   
**Who Qualifies for this leave?**  
 

> * Any woman employee in the government service, regardless of employment status, who is a victim of violence as defined under Section 3 of R.A. 9262: physical, sexual, psychological harm or suffering, or economic abuse, including threats, battery, assault, coercion, harassment or arbitrary deprivation of liberty, committed by her husband, former husband, or a man with whom she has a common child.

 

> * Any woman employee whose child is a victim of such violence, where the child is below 18, or 18 and above but unable to take care of himself/herself.

   
**Application Requirements:**  
 

> * Provide steps:

  1. Accomplish CS Form No. 6, checking “10-Day VAWC Leave” under Item 6.A.

  2. Attach any one of the following supporting documents:

     * Barangay Protection Order (BPO) obtained from the barangay;

     * Temporary or Permanent Protection Order (TPO/PPO) obtained from the court;

     * If no protection order has been issued yet, a certification from the Punong Barangay/Kagawad, the Prosecutor, or the Clerk of Court that an application for a BPO/TPO/PPO has been filed;

     * In the absence of the above, a police report specifying the details of the violence plus a medical certificate, accepted at the discretion of the immediate supervisor.

  3. File before the leave whenever practicable, or immediately upon return from the leave.

  4. If an extension is needed as specified in the protection order, file a separate application charged against other available leave benefits.

## **10\. Rehabilitation Privilege**

   
Leave with full pay for officials and employees who sustain wounds or injuries in the performance of official duty, so they can rest, recuperate and rehabilitate. *(Sec. 55, Rule XVI, implemented by CSC-DBM Joint Circular No. 1, s. 2006 and amended by CSC-DBM Joint Circular No. 1, s. 2015.)*  
   
**Key Benefits and Conditions**  
 

> * **Duration:** A maximum of 6 months with full pay. The duration, frequency and terms are based on the recommendation of medical authority. Availment may be for less than 6 months, on a half-time basis, or on an intermittent schedule, provided the cumulative total does not exceed 6 months.

 

> * **Service Requirement:** None as to length of service. Applies to personnel with permanent, temporary, casual or contractual appointments, including those with fixed terms of office.

 

> * **Usage:** For wounds and/or injuries sustained while in the performance of official duties, meaning the employee was already at work. It also covers accidents while engaged in activities inherent to the performance of duties, including Official Business outside the work station, Official Travel, authorized Overtime, Detail Orders, and Special Assignment Orders. Injuries from accidents while going to work and going home from work *may* be considered sustained in the performance of official duties (CSC-DBM Joint Circular No. 1, s. 2015). This is not automatic. The agency head has to weigh circumstantial factors such as the employee’s established intent, usual route, reasonable proximity to the workplace, and the timing of the injury.

 

> * **Limitations:** Absences during the rehabilitation period are **not deducted** from accumulated vacation or sick leave credits, but the employee **does not earn** vacation or sick leave credits while on rehabilitation privilege. Illness or sickness resulting from or aggravated by working conditions or the environment is **not** a basis for the privilege, even if compensable under ECC rules. The employee continues to receive salary and regular benefits (PERA, AdCom, year-end bonus, cash gift) but **not** benefits tied to actual performance of duties such as RATA. First-aid expenses are reimbursable, preferably in government facilities, subject to availability of funds and not exceeding ₱5,000.00 unless higher expenditures are certified as necessary by medical authorities and approved by the head of agency.

   
**Who Qualifies for this leave?**  
 

> * All personnel with permanent, temporary, casual or contractual appointments, including those with fixed terms of office, during their employment with the agency.

 

> * **Not covered:** Those engaged under contract of service or job order, since they are not government employees.

 

> * The agency head determines whether the injuries were incurred while in the performance of duties, and is personally liable for any false or fraudulent claims or irregular availment.

   
**Application Requirements:**  
 

> * Provide steps:

  1. File a letter application for the Rehabilitation Privilege within one (1) week from the time of the accident, except where a longer period is warranted. A member of the injured employee’s immediate family may file on his/her behalf.

  2. Accomplish CS Form No. 6, checking “Rehabilitation Privilege” under Item 6.A.

  3. Attach the relevant reports: the police report (if any) and a medical certificate stating the nature of the injuries, the course of treatment, and the need to undergo rest, recuperation and rehabilitation.

  4. If the attending physician is a private practitioner, obtain the written concurrence of a government physician, particularly on the duration of the rehabilitation period.

  5. Submit to the agency head, who determines whether the injuries were duty-related and approves the privilege.

  6. For first-aid reimbursement, submit the corresponding expense documents for approval, subject to the ₱5,000 ceiling.  
     * Depending on the management/agency.

  7. The agency head, through the agency medical staff and/or the HRMO, monitors the employee’s medical status monthly during the rehabilitation period.

## **11\. Special Leave Benefits for Women**

> * **Usage:** Covers the recuperation period following surgery. Earned leave credits may be used for the pre-surgery preparation period; where sick leave credits are exhausted, vacation leave credits may be used.

> * List of illness:  
  * Refer to CSC MC No. 25, s. 2010\.

   
**Who Qualifies for this leave?**

  4. Submit for the recommendation of the immediate supervisor and the approval of the head of agency.

  5. Upon return, submit a certificate of fitness to work issued by the attending physician.

  5. Submit for HR certification, recommendation and approval following the standard routing on CS Form No. 6\.

No. 11642 (Domestic Administrative Adoption and Alternative Child Care Act, 2022\) moved domestic adoption from the courts to an administrative process under the National Authority for Child Care (NACC). ## **15\. Monetization of Leave Credits**

   
Payment in advance, under prescribed limits and conditions, of the money value of an employee’s leave credits upon request, without the employee actually going on leave.  
   
**Key Benefits and Conditions**  
 

> * **Duration / Amount:** A minimum of 10 days may be monetized, provided at least 5 days of vacation leave are retained after monetization, and a maximum of 30 days may be monetized in a given year. Monetization of 50% or more of accumulated leave credits may be allowed for valid and justifiable reasons.

> * **Service Requirement:** The employee must have accumulated at least 15 days of vacation leave credits.

> * **Usage:** Computed using either of the following formulas:

  * Monthly Salary × No. of days to be monetized × 0.0481927 (constant factor) \= money value, 

  or

  * (Monthly Salary ÷ 20.916667) × No. of days to be monetized \= money value.

> * **Limitations:** Monetization of 50% or more requires the favorable recommendation of the agency head and is subject to the availability of funds. Employees who monetize 10 days out of 15 days of accumulated vacation leave are still required to go on the 5-day mandatory/forced leave.

   
**Who Qualifies for this leave?**  
 

> * Officials and employees in both the career and non-career service, whether permanent, temporary, casual or coterminous, who have accumulated at least 15 days of vacation leave credits.

 

> * For monetization of 50% or more, the reason must fall under: health, medical and hospital needs of the employee or immediate family; financial aid brought about by force majeure events such as calamities, typhoons, fire, earthquake and accidents affecting the life, limb and property of the employee or immediate family; educational needs of the employee or immediate family; payment of mortgages and loans entered into for the benefit of the employee or immediate family; extreme financial need where present income is insufficient for basic needs such as food, shelter and clothing; or other analogous cases as determined by the Commission.

   
**Application Requirements:**  
 

> * Provide steps:

  1. Accomplish CS Form No. 6, and under Item 6.B (“Other purpose”) tick Monetization of Leave Credits.

  2. Indicate the number of days to be monetized, the 5-day retained balance.

  3. For monetization of 50% or more, attach documentation substantiating the valid and justifiable reason (medical records, proof of calamity damage, tuition assessments, loan or mortgage documents, etc.).

  4. Secure the favorable recommendation of the agency head where monetization of 50% or more is requested.

  5. HR certifies the leave credit balance under Item 7.A and computes the money value using the prescribed formula.

  6. Approval is subject to the availability of funds.

## **16\. Terminal Leave**

   
Application for the commutation of all accumulated vacation and sick leave credits by an official or employee who is severing his or her connection with the government.  
   
**Key Benefits and Conditions**  
 

> * **Duration / Amount:** Commutation of all accumulated vacation and sick leave credits, exclusive of Saturdays, Sundays and holidays, without limitation as to number of days and regardless of when the credits were earned.

 

> * **Service Requirement:** Resignation, retirement or separation from the service is a *condition sine qua non*. It must first be shown that public employment has ceased by one of these modes of severance.

 

> * **Usage:** Computed as TLB \= S × D × CF, where S is the highest monthly salary received at any time during the period of government employment (not necessarily the latest salary), D is the number of accumulated vacation and sick leave credits, and CF is the constant factor 0.0481927 (derived from 12 ÷ 251 working days per year).

 

> * **Limitations:** Requests for payment must be brought within ten (10) years from the time the right of action accrues. An official or employee on terminal leave **does not earn** leave credits, being already out of the service. Clearance from the Ombudsman is **not** required for processing and payment of terminal leave (it is needed only for retirement benefits). Barangay officials are **not** entitled to terminal leave benefits under CSC-DBM Joint Circular No. 1, s. 2004, since their credits are cumulated for one year only and commuted yearly.

   
**Who Qualifies for this leave?**  
 

> * Any official or employee of the government who retires, voluntarily resigns, is allowed to resign, or is separated from the service through no fault of his or her own.

 

> * Where a person whose leaves were commuted upon separation is re-employed in government before the expiration of the commuted leave, no refund of the money value is required, but leave credits restart from a zero balance.

> 

> * For transfer, at least 1 year para ma receive sa receiving agency.

>   
   
**Application Requirements:**  
 

> * Provide steps:

  1. Effect the severance first: resignation, retirement, or separation from the service.

  2. Accomplish CS Form No. 6, and under Item 6.B (“Other purpose”) tick Terminal Leave; indicate Requested under Item 6.D (Commutation).

  3. Attach proof of severance (approved resignation letter, retirement papers, or notice of separation) and the agency clearance.

  4. Send the application for commutation of vacation and sick leave to the head of department concerned for approval. Ombudsman clearance is not required for this purpose.

  5. HR certifies the total accumulated vacation and sick leave credits and identifies the highest monthly salary received during the employee’s government service.


  6. Service Record of the Employee.


  7. GSIS Clearance of the Employee.

  8. Compute the benefit as TLB \= S × D × 0.0481927 and process payment.

  9. File the request for payment within 10 years from the accrual of the right of action.  
     * Applicable to separated employee before 2020\.

## **17\. Leave Without Pay (LWOP)**

   
Not a checkbox on CS Form No. 6, but the outcome recorded under Item 7.C (“\_\_\_\_\_ days without pay”) whenever an absence exceeds the employee’s earned credits. Included here because any leave module has to handle it.  
   
**Key Benefits and Conditions**  
 

> * **Duration:** LWOP not exceeding one (1) year may be granted, in addition to vacation and sick leave earned. LWOP in excess of one month requires the clearance of the proper head of department or agency.

 

> * **Service Requirement:** None; it applies whenever earned credits are exhausted.

 

> * **Usage:** All absences in excess of accumulated vacation or sick leave credits are without pay. Salary is computed as: Salary \= Monthly Salary − \[(Monthly Salary ÷ Calendar days) × No. of days of LWOP\].

 

> * **Limitations:** LWOP shall not be granted whenever the employee still has leave with pay to his credit, except in the case of secondment. For step increment purposes, approved vacation LWOP aggregating 15 days or less within the three-year period does not interrupt the continuity of the service requirement; beyond 15 days, the step increment is delayed by the same number of days. An employee on LWOP who fails to report for work at the expiration of one year is considered automatically separated. A seconded employee is on LWOP from the mother agency for the duration of the secondment but still earns leave credits, commutable and payable by the receiving agency.

   
**Who Qualifies for this leave?**  
 

> * Any official or employee whose absence exceeds the vacation or sick leave credits earned.

 

> * Seconded employees, for the duration of the secondment, even where they still have leave with pay to their credit.

 

> * An official or employee with a pending administrative case is not barred from enjoying leave privileges.

   
**Application Requirements:**  
 

> * Provide steps:

  1. Accomplish CS Form No. 6 for the underlying leave type (vacation or sick).

  2. Where the absence exceeds available credits, the excess is approved under Item 7.C as “\_\_\_\_\_ days without pay”.

  3. For LWOP in excess of one month, secure the clearance of the proper head of department or agency.

  4. Report back for work before the expiration of one year from the start of the LWOP, or be considered automatically separated from the service.

## **Common Rules Across All Leave Types**

 

> * **Form:** All leave applications use CS Form No. 6 (Revised 2020). Maternity leave allocation also uses CS Form No. 6a, s. 2020\. 

>  

> * **Medical certificates:** May be issued by a government or non-government physician in any form, provided the certificate bears the complete details of the physician (name and PRC/license, PTR and S2 numbers), the employee, and the employee’s condition or state of health.  
  * For rehab, medical certificate must be issued by the public physician.

 

> * **Deemed approved:** Where an application for leave, including terminal leave, is not acted upon by the head of agency or authorized representative within 5 working days from receipt, the application is deemed approved.

 

> * **Unauthorized leave:** An employee absent without approved leave is not entitled to salary for that period, but the absence is no longer deducted from accumulated leave credits.

 

> * **Pending administrative case:** Does not bar an official or employee from enjoying leave privileges.

 

> * **Accumulation:** Vacation and sick leave are cumulative; any portion not taken within the calendar year is carried over to succeeding years without limit (except where covered by a special law).

 

> * **Actual service basis:** Vacation and sick leave are computed at 1 day each for every 24 days of actual service, using the CSC tables of computation.

 

> * **Transfer:** Leave credits transfer with the employee when moving from one government agency to another; a remedy is available where the transfer of credits is denied.

 

> * **Disciplinary action:** Any violation of leave laws, rules or regulations is a cause for disciplinary action.
