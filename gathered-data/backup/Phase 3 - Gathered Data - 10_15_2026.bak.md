NOSI

* Effectivity of NOSI will be adjusted or moved forward if the employee has an LWOP of 15 or more. The basis of the adjusted period will be actual date of NOSI \+ No. of LWOP Days.

* Example:

  * NOSI Effectivity: Jan. 15, 2026  
  * No. of LWOP Days: 30\.  
  * Adjusted NOSI Effectivity \= Feb. 14, 2026
